How to isolate the leak

  1. Make the phone rigid. Use a tripod, clamp, or heavy support. Camera movement will look like ripples.
  2. Light the puddle from the side. Reflections make ripple rings easier to detect. Avoid flickering lamps.
  3. Select only still water. Exclude pump vibration, hoses, shadows, and flowing edges.
  4. Calibrate for 5 seconds while nothing changes. Then start detection.
  5. Run one state at a time: all pumps off, Pump 1 only, Pump 2 only, inlet water only, and combinations.
  6. Compare intervals. A large change immediately after a state change points toward that machine, pipe, joint, or pressure condition.

Safety: do not enter or lean into a sewage pit. Toxic gases, pathogens, electrical equipment, and confined-space hazards can be fatal. Keep the phone and yourself outside the pit.
